## What's New in v0.1.5

### 1. RevenueCat Subscription & Monetization Architecture
* **Full RevenueCat SDK Integration:** Integrated `react-native-purchases` for production-grade in-app subscription lifecycle management.
* **Tier Architecture:**
  - **Community Tier ($0/forever):** 100% on-device SQLite storage, offline access, standard Markdown export, BYOK for Gemini/Groq, and 3 weekly Serendipity Engine scans.
  - **MindMesh Pro ($4.16/mo billed annually or $9.99/mo):** Unlimited continuous background Serendipity scans, automated PRD and paywall schema generation, multi-device Obsidian/Notion sync, and priority multi-modal synthesis.
* **7-Day Free Trial:** Zero barrier to entry with 1-click subscription activation and transparent cancel/restore anytime policies.
* **Multi-Device Entitlement Restoration:** Single-tap restore entitlement flow validating cached and remote RevenueCat entitlements across Android devices.

### 2. Quiet Minimalist Luxury Redesign (Burgundy Pop Accent)
* **Obsidian Canvas:** Purged all obsolete white backgrounds (`#F7F7F8` / `#FFFFFF`) and screaming cyber/neon palettes. All screens now adhere to a quiet `#030308` / `#090912` dark surface.
* **Signature Burgundy Pop (`#8B1A2B` / `#B8334F`):** Elegant wine-burgundy accents applied deliberately to active step indicators, plan cards, upgrade badges, and CTA action buttons.
* **Zero Emojis Policy:** Pure typography and crisp Lucide vector icons (`Crown`, `CheckCircle2`, `Zap`, `Sparkles`, `X`, `ArrowRight`, `ShieldCheck`).
* **Cleaned Visual Geometry:** Removed background glow circular artifacts for crisp, distraction-free legibility.

### 3. Web Platform Parity
* Updated `SubscriptionPricingSection` on the companion Next.js web application to mirror the obsidian canvas and Burgundy accents.
* Live and globally distributed on Vercel at [https://mindsmeshai.vercel.app/#pricing](https://mindsmeshai.vercel.app/#pricing).
