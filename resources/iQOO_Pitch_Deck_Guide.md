## 💡 Quick Tips for Designing Your Slides in Canva / Google Slides / Keynote

1. **Keep Text Punchy:** Use the headlines and bold bullet points above. Avoid giant walls of unformatted text.
2. **Visual Mockups:** Use clean phone mockups (frame the app interface inside a modern smartphone bezel).
3. **Use the Badges:** Add the `Snapdragon Hexagon NPU` and `Office Kit Bridge` tags prominently on Slides 1, 4, 5, and 7 to catch the jury's eyes immediately.
4. **Export Settings:** In Canva or Google Slides, export as **Standard PDF** (or compress if needed) to ensure the file size remains well below the 25 MB upload limit.
