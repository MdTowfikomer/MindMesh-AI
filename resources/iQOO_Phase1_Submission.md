### 2. Description
*(Requirement: Min 50 characters / words)*

```markdown
Every week, builders and knowledge workers capture dozens of whiteboard photos, voice notes, code snippets, and research tabs. These fragments sit siloed in camera rolls and note apps—never connected, never executed. MindMesh AI solves this cognitive fragmentation by creating an on-device, dual-device productivity bridge powered by the iQOO flagship phone and Office Kit.

MindMesh leverages the iQOO phone as a high-frequency sensory capture engine:
1. Creative Phone Hardware: Captures physical whiteboards and documents via instant Camera OCR, records on-the-go thoughts via floating Voice Memos, and runs local vector embeddings directly on the Snapdragon Hexagon NPU for zero-latency, 100% offline privacy.
2. Office Kit Dual-Device Bridge: Bridges research and deep work seamlessly between phone and laptop. Copying snippets on the laptop syncs directly into the iQOO phone's semantic memory; discovered patterns push back to the laptop workspace.
3. Serendipity Engine: Instead of static search, MindMesh continuously clusters disparate thoughts (e.g., a pricing screenshot + a voice note) to surface Actionable Guidance with 3 Concrete Next Steps, Explainability citations ("Why"), and a Hallmark Anti-AI-Slop quality verification gate.

Built specifically to thrive in the hackathon's Red Light (100% offline on-device NPU) and Green Light (seamless Office Kit laptop acceleration) windows, MindMesh transforms scattered chaos into clear, high-leverage execution.
```
